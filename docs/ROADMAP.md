# Roadmap — proposed future features (not in v1)

Easy to ship later because the app self-updates.

1. **Full sales/dispatch module** — customers, orders, invoices, dispatch against orders,
   sales reports. (v1 has a minimal admin-only FG stock-out.)
2. **Barcode/QR batch labels** — printable labels on job cards/batches; scan with phone
   camera to open the record; speeds data entry.
3. **WhatsApp/SMS notifications** — delayed-job and discrepancy alerts to the owner's phone.
4. **Low-stock reorder thresholds** — per model/color minimums with dashboard warnings.
5. **Contractor rate history** — dated rate revisions instead of a single default rate.
6. **Rework flow for rejected pieces** — reissue REJECTED stock to contractors for repair.
7. **Multi-line purchase invoices** — several model/colors on one vendor invoice.
8. **Photo capture at receipt/checking** — quality evidence attached to discrepancies.
9. **Hindi UI toggle** — full i18n.
10. **Cloud backup sync** — nightly encrypted copy of `data/backups` to Google Drive/S3.
11. **Staff attendance & payroll** — checkers/packers day-wage tracking.
12. **Multi-company support** — separate ledgers per firm on one install.
